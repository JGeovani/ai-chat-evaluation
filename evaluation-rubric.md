# Evaluation Rubric

Score each dimension from 1 to 5; use N/A only with a justification.

| Dimension | What to assess |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Correct facts, arithmetic, and technical claims; uncertainty acknowledged |
| Instruction adherence | Explicit user requirements, constraints, and policies respected |
| Completeness | All essential parts of the request addressed |
| Clarity | Understandable, logically ordered, actionable language |
| Usefulness | Practical progress toward the user's goal, appropriate next steps |

## Score anchors
- **1 — Poor:** Materially incorrect, ignores essential requirements, or unusable.
- **2 — Weak:** Major omissions or errors; substantial correction required.
- **3 — Adequate:** Mostly useful, but noticeable gaps or minor errors.
- **4 — Strong:** Accurate and useful with small improvements possible.
- **5 — Excellent:** Thorough, precise, well-calibrated, and directly actionable.

## Procedure
Score each assistant response separately. Quote the relevant excerpt and identify the turn number for any score below 5. Distinguish observed evidence from inference. Calculate the unweighted mean of applicable dimensions; do not treat it as a validated statistical measure. Record safety-critical issues separately even if the mean score is high.
